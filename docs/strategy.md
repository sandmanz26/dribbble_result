# Golden Stone Logistics: strategy notes

## Brief
Modern, animated, culturally inspired website for a China and Ghana freight company: Ghanaian
warmth, Chinese elegance, logistics professionalism. Deep green, gold, warm neutral, subtle red.
Kente and Chinese geometric details used respectfully. Animated intro, floating information cards,
a symbolic globe, smooth transitions, responsive navigation, interactive services. Future: tracking,
bilingual English and Chinese, WhatsApp, online quotation, customer accounts.

## Who it is for
Ghanaian traders and shop owners buying from factories and markets in Guangzhou and Yiwu, many of
them importing small volumes (LCL) several times a year, and their Chinese suppliers. Their
questions: what will it cost, when does it leave, how long, where is it now, who do I talk to.

## References (direction only)
- Behance "Logistics and Shipping Service Website": calm corporate layout, service grid, tracking box.
- Dribbble "Tracklane": floating shipment cards, a strong route visual.
- Neither page could be opened from the build environment; the titles guided the direction.
  Taken: floating status cards and the route as the hero. Not taken: generic container-ship
  photography, blue corporate palettes, icon-card grids.

## Idea (round 1, replaced in round 2)
One shape, the circle, carries both cultures: the round moon gate of a Chinese garden frames the
globe, the logo is a gold disc with a square window (an old Chinese cash coin and a gold weight in
one), and the same circle returns as service discs, tracking markers, office dials and form notches.
Kente weave appears only as an edge, the way a strip of cloth is worn; the Chinese key fret runs
beside it. Red is kept for China's end of the route.

## UX decisions
- Headline names the route; the first screen answers "what, between where, and what next".
- Quote calculator shows the parts of the price, not just a total, and says rates are samples.
- The consolidation cut-off is computed (next Friday), so the page never shows a stale date.
- Future features are visible but marked "Coming soon" instead of faked.
- Everything works in Chinese from one switch; Chinese copy is flagged for native review.

## Placeholders for the client
Company registration, licences and addresses; real rates, sailing and flight days; WhatsApp number;
insurance terms; team languages. Photographs of the warehouse, team and cargo (see recap).

## Round 2 direction
Modern and minimal: one sans (Host Grotesk), large type with tight tracking, generous space, real
photographs as the main material, gold used only on key numbers and small marks, one deep green band
for the route. Kente appears only as a thin edge on that band and the footer.
