## Roast before generating images

# Image plan and roast (before generating)

Budget 30 credits. Plan: 14 medium images (about 7 credits). The client's own device photo and carton render are used as real assets and as the reference for every device shot.

## Plan
1. Spearmint device 3/4 on a flat Spearmint field (PDP, shop card)
2. Arctic Mint device 3/4 on a flat Arctic field
3. Spearmint device twisted open, nozzle up, fine mist (twist story, PDP)
4. Pocket scale: Arctic device with keys, earbuds case, lipstick, card wallet, overhead on stone
5. In hand: Spearmint device at chest height, city evening behind
6-11. Six Moments cards (9:16): first hello, big pitch, third coffee (device on desk), slow dance, group photo, close talker
12. Squad gift: six devices in a kraft gift box
13. About: workbench with early device prototypes, hands only
14. Arctic device straight on with its carton (where to buy, hello page)

## Roast (risk, then fix)
- The device changes shape or loses its wordmark: every device shot passes the client photo as the reference and says "the exact device from the reference, same proportions, same vertical BRTH LABS wordmark". Reject any image where the type is garbled.
- Brief bans glows, streaks and CGI renders (the carton render has light streaks): ask for real studio photography, flat matte backdrop, soft single light, "no light streaks, no glow, no lens flare".
- Field colours drift (the generator makes green emerald or neon): name the hex and say "flat deep green, desaturated, like the device".
- People look staged or uncanny: candid, mid-moment, medium distance, natural light, diverse 18 to 45, no posing at camera.
- Moments that show bad breath or shame: the brief forbids it; every scene is the moment just before something good.
- Lime and sky are accents only: never as backgrounds in photos.
- Pack facts conflict: the client photo prints 0.39 fl oz while the brief says 10 mL = 0.34 fl oz. Keep generated devices close-up of the wordmark only, and flag the mismatch to the client.
- Mist reads as smoke: "fine, faint, almost invisible cool mist, a few droplets in the light".

## Open items for the client

- The device photo prints "0.39 FL OZ (10 mL)"; the brief says 10 mL = 0.34 fl oz. 10 mL is 0.34 fl oz, so the print needs checking.
- Comparison table rows, ingredient list and claims (sugar free, vegan, TSA) to be confirmed by the product team.
- Reviews, UGC handles and retailer list are placeholders until launch.
- The video lightbox is a still placeholder until the twist loop is shot.
- Discount codes FRESH15 and HELLO20 are samples.
